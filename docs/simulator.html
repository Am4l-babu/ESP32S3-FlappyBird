How to Play

Space / Click the OLED, or tap the TOUCH pad to flap.
One flap per press — exactly like the real TTP223 rising-edge detector. Clear the pipe gaps, don't hit the floor or ceiling caps.
Idle 5s on Title/Score → the firmware plays its wipe-and-spiral transition back to the title screen.
